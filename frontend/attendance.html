Attendance

Present excludes declared breaks. Active (timer) is time the annotation timer was running, so it is always lower than Present and the two will not agree. A timeout end means the tab was closed without logging out — the time shown is the last moment we saw them, a lower bound, not a logout time. Tasks touched is tasks the person had open; it is not tasks completed, which this system cannot attribute to a person. * marks a day containing a break entered after the fact.
